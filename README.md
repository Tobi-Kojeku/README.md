## Hi, I’m Tobi 👋
I am a Health Data Analyst with a **BSc in Statistics** and an **MSc in Public Health**. My work focuses on transforming health data into actionable evidence across **medical research**, **MEAL (Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning)**, and **healthcare service improvement**. I collaborate closely with researchers, clinicians, and hospital unit leaders—supporting statistical analysis, study design, and the interpretation of results for publication and service decision-making.
* 🌍  I'm based in New Zealand
* ✉️  You can contact me at [ade\_kojeku@yahoo.com](mailto:ade_kojeku@yahoo.com)
* 🧠  I'm currently learning AI in Digital Health

### Core focus
- **Research analytics:** descriptive, causal, predictive, and epidemiology-oriented analysis  
- **Clinical research support:** statistical analysis for protocol development, study design, and results discussion  
- **MEAL and monitoring:** indicator analysis, data quality/validation, and reporting to support learning and accountability  
- **Healthcare service improvement:** evaluating delivery performance and translating findings into recommendations  
- **Communication and visualisation:** clear, publication-ready tables, figures, and narratives for stakeholders

### Tools and methods
I use **REDCap**, **SQL (via DBeaver)**, **Stata**, **Python**, **R**, **Power BI**, and **NVivo** to build reproducible workflows for data preparation, analysis, and reporting. I emphasize robust data validation, ethical handling of sensitive information (de-identification/aggregation), and transparent methodology.
